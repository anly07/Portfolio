Privacy Policy

Last updated:

What this site collects

This is a personal portfolio site. Data is only collected when you actively submit one of the forms on the site:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, and message, so I can reply to your enquiry.
  • Feedback form: your name, star rating, and comment. Approved feedback is displayed publicly in the Testimonials section of the site.
  • Basic, anonymous usage data: page visits and a few interaction events (e.g. which button was clicked), tied to a random identifier generated in your browser — not to your name or email, and not shared with any advertiser or analytics network.

How it's stored and used

Form submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the site owner, and contact form messages are additionally emailed directly to the owner. This data is used solely to respond to enquiries and to display testimonials on this site. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

Your choices

You can ask for your submitted contact or feedback entry to be removed at any time by using the contact form to request it.

Contact

For any privacy questions, please use the contact form on the homepage — it reaches the site owner directly.
