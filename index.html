FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about design, front-end development, dashboards and working together.

What services do you offer as a product designer and front-end developer?

I'm Ainsley C., a product designer and front-end developer. I offer UI/UX design, Figma design systems and front-end development with React and Next.js, from the first wireframe to a responsive, working interface. Because I design the screens and build them in code, nothing gets lost between design and development.

What kinds of projects have you built?

My work includes task management systems for creating, assigning, prioritising and tracking tasks, a bike management app that tracks fuel, mileage, maintenance and expenses, energy management interfaces, and Figma design systems. Each project in the Projects section lists the technologies used and, where available, a live link and GitHub repository.

Can you design and build real-time dashboards and monitoring systems?

Yes. Real-time dashboards and monitoring systems are a core part of what I do. I design the data-heavy screens in Figma, then build them with React and Next.js so live numbers, statuses and charts stay clear, fast and easy to read on any device.

Which technologies and design tools do you use?

For design I use Figma for UI/UX, wireframes and design systems. For development I use React and Next.js with Tailwind CSS, plus motion design for small interaction details. Turning a Figma design system into reusable code components is a regular part of my workflow.

Can you improve the SEO and speed of my website?

Yes. SEO optimisation is part of how I build websites: semantic HTML, clear headings, descriptive meta titles and descriptions, structured data such as the FAQ markup on this page, fast-loading pages and mobile-friendly layouts, so search engines can understand and rank your content. If you already have a site, get in touch and I can look at where it can improve.

Are you available for freelance work, and how do I contact you?

Yes, I'm currently open to freelance projects. The quickest way to reach me is the contact form in the Contact section of this page: tell me what you're building and I'll reply by email. You can also connect with me on LinkedIn.
